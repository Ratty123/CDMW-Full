Repeated table loading, a custom mesh on an existing inventory definition, private filenames alone, and a green mock-registry test do not establish the goal.

Start with a small item family and startup-only activation, then expand. Startup-only still requires normal cache eviction/rematerialization and saved identity. Do not silently exclude a required dependency to mark the full feature complete.
